About us

Your laundry, treated like our own.

Alexis Laundry Services exists to give you back the hours you'd rather spend elsewhere. We collect your laundry from your door, treat every load with the care its label asks for — from everyday shirts to delicate silks — and bring it back clean, pressed, and folded.

Every order is weighed, tagged, and washed separately, so what leaves your home is exactly what comes back to it. We sort by colour, fabric, and care instructions before a single item touches a machine, and use detergents that are gentle on skin and kind to fabric.

Whether it's a single bag of everyday wear or a full household changeover of bedding and towels, we run the same routine: careful pickup, careful wash, careful return.
